Automation · Hiring

Automated applicant screening funnel

Problem

Job applications landed in a spreadsheet and sat there — screening was manual, slow, and inconsistent between reviewers, and strong candidates waited days for a first look.

Built

An end-to-end screening pipeline: a skills-test application form feeds a webhook, every submission is graded against a locked rubric by an AI scorer, and a candidate card — score, verdict, biggest strength and weakness, and a hire recommendation with a one-tap link to the full submission — lands in the hiring channel within a minute of applying.

QA · Coaching · Automation

Automated QA & coaching system

Problem

Performance reviews were ad-hoc and verbal — no scoring rubric, no documented feedback, no way to track whether coaching was landing or patterns were repeating across agents.

Built

A structured QA framework: conversations graded against a locked rubric on fixed scoring bands, a strike ledger tied to a progressive discipline policy, and per-agent Slack feedback cards drafted automatically by an AI-powered first-pass pipeline. Supervisors review and approve before anything reaches the agent. Prior audit results are carried forward so recurring patterns are flagged as repeats, not treated as new.

n8nAI (Claude Opus)SlackSOPsGoogle Sheets
Every agent gets structured, documented feedback. Patterns caught before they become habits.
